Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

📷

Photo-based logging

Snap any meal and get full macros instantly. No barcodes, no food database, no manual entry. Point and shoot.

⏱️

Exercise time cost

Every meal tells you exactly how long your chosen exercise would need to run to offset it. Makes food choices feel real and personal.

🩸

Blood sugar prediction

A 2-hour blood glucose curve after every meal. Ideal for diabetics, pre-diabetics, or anyone who wants to feel better after eating.

📈

Macro trends

Weekly reports showing where you nailed your goals and where you went off track — so you can build real habits, not just log meals.

🍽️

Food recommendations

Struggling to hit protein? Under on carbs? We suggest exactly what to add to your next meal to stay on track toward your goal.

🎯

Goal-aligned targets

Lose weight, maintain, build muscle — your macro targets and food guidance automatically adjust to what you're working toward.
